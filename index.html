About ACCI

A Legacy of Unity, Commerce & Community

Agrawal Chamber of Commerce and Industries (ACCI), Jabalpur is more than a business directory — it is the heartbeat of the Agrawal community. Founded on principles of mutual trust, shared prosperity, and collective growth, ACCI has been the backbone of business networking and community welfare in Jabalpur for over two decades.

Whether you're a first-generation entrepreneur or a third-generation manufacturer — ACCI is your community, your support system, and your proudest address.
